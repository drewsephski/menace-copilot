Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 9, 2026

What Menace Agent is

Menace Agent is a macOS desktop application that captures screen and audio from your computer to provide real-time conversation assistance. It is sold as downloadable software with optional subscription access.

Data we process

  • On your device: Audio and screen content are processed locally for transcription (Whisper). Session history and settings are stored in your Mac’s Application Support folder.
  • AI requests: When you use included AI, prompts and context may be sent to our AI provider to generate answers. If you supply your own API key, requests go to that provider under their terms.
  • Payments: Checkout and billing are handled by Polar and Stripe. We do not store full payment card numbers.
  • License keys: We validate license keys with Polar to confirm your subscription or pass.

What we don’t do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your call audio or screen content for advertising.

Your choices

You can delete local app data by removing the app and its Application Support folder. For billing or license issues, contact support.
